The Godrej POP Safes offer high-precision dual-controlling locks, providing outstanding security features including corrosion resistance and excellent lock strength up to 7000N per bolt pair. They are available in various colors like grey, black, and white powder coated, removable magnetic finish (mobi) and enable weight options for easy installation.
