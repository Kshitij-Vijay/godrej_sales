Living Roam/Soto Bed 7 Printerio is a living room furniture set consisting of a sofa bed, tabletop with an attached printer holder (which holds the printer), and side tables. It was produced by Living Roam to cater to families who need more space and functionality for their entertainment needs. This unique design provides users with ample seating options while also providing a convenient place for printing documents or sending emails using a built-in printer module.
